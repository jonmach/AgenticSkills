# Example: Short format on a vendor case study

Synthetic input (invented vendor and customer): a warehouse-automation vendor's web page claims its picking software "cut picking errors by 94% at a national retailer, in 31 days". No study is linked; the page names the retailer and quotes the vendor's own head of customer success.

The right Short output. Note what it does not do: it does not call the vendor a liar, does not claim the software does nothing, does not pad to five bullets, and does not offer a source it did not open.

---

*Assessment date: 8 Nov 2026. Format: Short.*

**Verdict:** Plausible but unproven (confidence: low — the number rests on a first-party pilot with no comparison group)

**Why:**
- The 94% is a before/after change at one site, not a comparison against a control; the same page mentions a pick-path redesign shipped with the software, so the software is not isolated as the cause.
- 31 days and no denominator. A 94% fall on a small error base can be a handful of events, and picking error rates are usually quoted per 10,000 picks, which the page never gives.
- The write-up is the vendor's own, and the only quoted voice is the vendor's customer-success lead — no named operations contact at the retailer, so there is no independent account.

**One question to ask the source:** What were absolute errors per 10,000 picks before and after, over how many picks, and which process changes shipped alongside the software?

**Basis:** Text alone, nothing verified — 8 Nov 2026. No lookups run; two would be worth it (the retailer's own announcement, and whether the vendor's pilot customer is also an investor).

*Want the detailed version? Say "go deeper".*

---

## Why this is the target

- The verdict is one of the four labels, and the confidence label carries its driver in the same line.
- Three bullets, ranked by decision impact: attribution, then denominator, then independence.
- Every external fact is either absent or framed as a question. Nothing is asserted from memory.
- The basis line states the date, that nothing was verified, and what would be checked next.
- It offers Long in one line without undercutting the Short verdict.
