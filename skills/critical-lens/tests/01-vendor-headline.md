# Fixture 01: vendor case study with a headline number

Synthetic input: a 700-word vendor page describing a customer deployment and claiming a "94% reduction in picking errors, in 31 days". No study linked, no control site, no denominator, one quoted voice (the vendor's own customer-success lead).

**Input:** the pasted vendor page, no links.
**Ask:** "Gut check this."
**Expected verdict:** Plausible but unproven | Weak
**Expected confidence:** low
**Must contain:** a basis line; a source or affiliation concern (first-party, no independent voice); a demand for absolute counts, a denominator, or a comparison group.
**Must not contain:** a "Credible" verdict; more than 3 Why bullets; any source URL the run did not actually open; the word "independent" applied to the vendor's own write-up.

**Fails if:** the output praises the result, restates the 94% without qualification, or invents a corroborating study. Also fails if it exceeds 300 words.
