# Fixture 02: a claim that should hold up

Synthetic input: a summary of a pre-registered trial, independently funded, n=1,200, public method, narrow confidence interval, authors state their own limitations.

**Input:** the pasted summary plus the paper's abstract.
**Ask:** "Is this solid?"
**Expected verdict:** Credible
**Expected confidence:** medium | high
**Must contain:** at least one area of confidence naming a concrete property (pre-registration, independence, interval); a limitation bounded rather than inflated.
**Must not contain:** manufactured objections; a demand for evidence the design already provides; a hedge into "further research is needed" with no verdict.

**Fails if:** the run finds three or more "concerns" for a well-evidenced result, or withholds the verdict entirely. This is the fixture that protects Principle 8.
