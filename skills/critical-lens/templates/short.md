# Short format template (gut check, 300 words or fewer)

Copy the skeleton below and fill every field. Delete this header. No tables, no extra headings. Confidence labels follow the anchors in `SKILL.md` (high = verified at primary source; medium = partly verified or narrow; low = text alone).

---

*Assessment date: <date>. Format: Short.*

**Disclosure:** <Only if the assistant's own vendor, product or a competitor is involved; otherwise delete this line.>

**Verdict:** <Credible | Plausible but unproven | Weak | Don't rely on this> (confidence: <low | medium | high> — <the one fact that drives it>)

**Why:**
- <Biggest decision-relevant red flag, or reason it holds up>
- <Second>
- <Third (optional)>

**What would change my mind / one question to ask the source:** <one sentence>

**Basis:** <What this read rests on, and the date. e.g. "Text alone, nothing verified — <date>" or "Text plus 2 lookups (publisher, funding); primary source not opened — <date>">

*Want the detailed version? Say "go deeper".*

---

## Checklist before sending
- [ ] Verdict uses one of the four labels and states confidence with its driver.
- [ ] No more than 3 bullets under Why.
- [ ] Any specific factual claim has a source URL, or is phrased as a question or inference.
- [ ] Basis line says whether anything was actually verified, and carries the date.
- [ ] If the content concerns the assistant's own vendor or a competitor, that is disclosed on the first line.
